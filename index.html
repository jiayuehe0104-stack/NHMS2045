User Licence Agreement (Summary)

  1. Once the device is activated, the system will collect your physiological data around the clock, including but not limited to: heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen levels, respiratory rate, body temperature, sleep status, neural activity indicators, emotional stress index, and other health metrics added following system updates.

    The device runs continuously by default and does not need to be switched on manually.

  2. The system will carry out a dynamic analysis of your health status by combining your real-time data, historical medical records and national medical databases, and will generate a personalised health risk rating.

    The risk assessment results will be continuously updated and used for: disease early warning, medical advice, health interventions and determining the priority of emergency care.

  3. By default, only the following authorised parties may view your health data:
    ✓ Yourself
    ✓ Healthcare organisations providing you with medical services
    ✓ Family members you have actively linked

    Unless otherwise provided for by law, no organisation may directly access your personal health data.

  4. Should the system determine that you are in a life-threatening situation and unable to respond actively, you authorise the following:
    · Automatic contact with the nearest medical facility;
    · Sharing of necessary health data with the emergency response team;
    · Remote activation of medical devices for emergency nerve stimulation and life support;
    · Transmission of your real-time location to the emergency services.

    The above measures do not require your immediate consent to be sought again.

  5. The National Health Bureau undertakes that:
    · Your health data will be stored in accordance with national encryption standards;
    · Unauthorised access to data will be subject to legal action.

    Nevertheless, no digital system can guarantee the complete absence of cybersecurity risks.

  6. As medical research progresses, new monitoring indicators, risk models or healthcare services may be added to the system.
    By continuing to use this system, you are deemed to have accepted such updates.
